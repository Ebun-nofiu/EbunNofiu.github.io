About Me

I've always been more interested in the question behind the numbers than the numbers themselves.

What's driving the drop in revenue? Where is a process breaking down? What are customers actually doing? And, perhaps most importantly, what should the business do about it?

That curiosity is what led me from Sociology into data and business analytics. Since then, I've worked across legal operations, hospitality, consulting, and business analysis, using data to make messy information easier to understand and decisions easier to make.

I work primarily with Excel, Power BI, SQL, and Python, but the tools are only part of it. I care about asking the right questions, finding what the data is actually saying, and communicating it in a way that makes sense to the person who has to act on it.

I'm currently a Business and Data Analyst and pursuing an MSc in Business Analytics at Quantic School of Business and Technology. This portfolio is a collection of the problems I've explored, the things I've built, and what I've learned along the way.
